💌 MY LETTER

Dear Gimi…

My Love, ❤️

I’ve been thinking about what I could write for your birthday, and honestly, I don’t think words will ever be enough to explain what you mean to me. Still, I want to try, because you deserve to know how deeply you are loved, appreciated, and cherished. 🥹🤍

When I look back at everything we’ve shared, I realize how many little moments have become some of the most precious memories of my life. The conversations, the laughter, the silly moments, the misunderstandings, the moments when we simply stayed there for each other — all of them mean something to me. 🌙🫶

You are beautiful, but it’s not just your smile, your eyes, or your hair that makes you beautiful. 🌸✨ It’s the person you are behind all of that. It’s your heart, your kindness, your thoughts, your way of caring, and the little things you do without even realizing how much they mean to someone.

I love your smile because it can make everything around you feel brighter. 😊❤️ I love your eyes because sometimes they say things that words never could. 👀✨ I love the way you think, the way you see the world, and the dreams you carry inside your heart. 🌷💫 And more than anything, I love the person you are when nobody is watching.

You have a heart that deserves to be protected and loved. 🤍 You care deeply, you feel deeply, and you have a kindness that makes you different. Sometimes I wish you could see yourself through my eyes, even for a moment, because maybe then you would understand just how special you really are. 🥹🌙

There will always be things in life that we cannot control. There will be difficult days, changes, distance, misunderstandings, and moments when things don’t go exactly the way we imagined. But through all of that, I hope you never forget one thing — you will always have a very special place in my heart. ❤️

I don’t want to love only the perfect parts of you. I want to know your fears, your dreams, your insecurities, your little habits, your random thoughts, and all those tiny things that make you you. 🫶🌸 I want to be there for your good days, but I also want to be someone you can lean on when the days aren’t so good.

Your birthday isn’t just a celebration of another year. 🎂✨ It is a reminder that the world became a little more beautiful because you were born. And somehow, my world became even more beautiful because our paths crossed. 🌎❤️

I hope this new year of your life gives you reasons to smile more, dreams worth chasing, people who appreciate your heart, and countless moments that make you genuinely happy. 🌷💫 I hope you become everything you dream of becoming, while never losing the beautiful person you already are.

And if someday you look back at this letter, I hope you remember how loved you were in this moment. 🥹🤍 Not because of how you looked, not because of what you could give me, but simply because you were you.

Thank you for being a part of my life. Thank you for every smile, every conversation, every memory, every little moment, and even every difficult one that taught us something. 🫶🌙

I don’t know exactly what the future has written for us, but I know one thing — whatever happens, I will always be grateful that I got to know you, love you, and call you mine. ❤️

Happy Birthday, my love. 🎂🎀❤️

May you always smile the way you do, dream without limits, love without fear, and remember just how precious you are. 🌸✨

With all my heart,
Always yours. ❤️
